VDR is a free download manager for Android. Paste a direct HTTP or HTTPS file URL — or a direct HLS .m3u8 / clear DASH .mpd playlist URL you have the right to download — and VDR fetches it with pause, resume, and retries. Optional Pro (one-time in-app purchase) unlocks page media scan, multi-URL batch queue, more download segments, and Focus Guard.

What it does
• Direct files — for example .mp4, .zip, .pdf, .apk, .ts, .m4s, audio, and .srt/.vtt subtitles.
• Optional HLS playlist download when you provide the playlist URL (.m3u8). Master playlists pick the highest-bandwidth variant; MPEG-TS or fMP4 (EXT-X-MAP) segments are concatenated into one file under Downloads/VDR/Videos.
• Optional clear DASH (.mpd) when you provide the manifest URL. DRM / ContentProtection manifests are rejected.
• Add a URL by typing it, pasting from the clipboard, or sharing text from your browser.
• Optional Wi-Fi only — downloads wait until you are on unmetered Wi-Fi.
• Finished files are saved under Downloads/VDR, grouped into Videos, Documents, Subtitles, Zips, Audio, Images, or Other.
• Pause and resume, including after the app is closed (a small sidecar keeps progress).
• Optional speed cap and Focus Guard (Focus Guard is part of optional Pro).

Pricing
VDR downloads from the Play Store for free. An optional one-time Pro unlock (product id vdr_pro) is offered in the app for advanced features. Purchases are handled by Google Play.
VDR does not extract streams from YouTube, Odysee, Netflix, or similar watch pages. It does not bypass DRM (Widevine/FairPlay) or decrypt commercial streams. It is not Internet Download Manager and does not claim IDM compatibility.

Privacy
No account, no ads, no analytics. Clipboard text is read on the device only to offer a URL. Network traffic goes only to the hosts of the URLs you start. Files stay in Downloads/VDR. See the in-listing privacy policy.

VDR is free software under the GNU GPL v3, ported from the desktop VDR project.
