App name (30 chars max): VDR

Short description (80 chars max):
Direct HTTP file downloads with resume, clipboard paste, and Wi-Fi only.

Full description:

VDR 1.1 is a free download manager for Android. Paste a direct HTTP or HTTPS file URL and VDR fetches it in parallel byte-range segments, with pause, resume, and retries.

What it does
• Direct files only — for example .mp4, .zip, .pdf, .apk. It will not fetch YouTube, Instagram, TikTok, or other watch/share pages; those links are rejected.
• Add a URL by typing it, pasting from the clipboard, or sharing text from your browser.
• Optional Wi-Fi only — downloads wait until you are on unmetered Wi-Fi.
• Finished files are saved under Downloads/VDR, grouped into Videos, Documents, Zips, Audio, Images, or Other so Files and Gallery can open them.
• Pause and resume, including after the app is closed (a small sidecar keeps progress).
• Optional speed cap so the rest of the phone stays usable.
• Focus Guard can pause on battery or Battery Saver, and slow transfers while you use the app.
• Progress shows in a notification.

What it does not do
VDR does not extract streams from YouTube or similar sites. It is not Internet Download Manager and does not claim IDM compatibility.

Privacy
No account, no ads, no analytics. Clipboard text is read on the device only to offer a URL. Network traffic goes only to the hosts of the URLs you start. Files stay in Downloads/VDR. See the in-listing privacy policy.

VDR is free software under the GNU GPL v3, ported from the desktop VDR project.
