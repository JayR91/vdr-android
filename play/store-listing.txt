VDR — Video Downloader for Android

VDR is a resumable download manager. It splits HTTP files into parallel
byte-range segments, pauses and resumes mid-transfer, retries failed
segments, and can cap bandwidth so the rest of the phone stays usable.

Focus Guard pauses downloads on battery or Battery Saver, and slows
transfers to 256 KB/s while you are using the app. Completed files are
sorted into Videos, Documents, Zips, Audio, Images, or Other.

Share a link from your browser into VDR, or paste a direct https file URL.

VDR is free software under the GNU GPL v3, ported from the desktop VDR
project. It downloads direct files only — it does not extract streams from
YouTube or other video sites.
