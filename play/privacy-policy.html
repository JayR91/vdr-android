VDR Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 August 2026

VDR is a local download manager. It does not create user accounts and does not include analytics or advertising SDKs.

Data the app processes

  • URLs you paste or share into the app, used only to fetch the file you requested.
  • Downloaded files and a small resume sidecar stored on the device.
  • Notification permission, used to show download progress.

What we do not collect

The app does not send your download list, files, or identifiers to a VDR server. Network traffic goes only to the hosts of the URLs you start.
