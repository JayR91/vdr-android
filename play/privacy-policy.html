VDR Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 August 2026

VDR (com.jayr91.vdr) version 1.1 is a local download manager for Android. It downloads direct HTTP(S) files only. It does not extract streams from YouTube or other watch pages. It does not create user accounts and does not include analytics, advertising, or crash-reporting SDKs.

Data the app processes on your device

  • URLs you paste, type, share into the app, or that VDR reads from the clipboard when you return to the app — used only to fetch the file you requested. Clipboard contents are not sent to a VDR server.
  • Downloaded files, stored in public storage under Downloads/VDR (with category subfolders). A small resume sidecar may be kept in app storage until the transfer finishes.
  • Notification permission, used only to show download progress in a foreground service notification.
  • Network type (metered vs Wi-Fi / unmetered), used only if you enable Wi-Fi only.

What we do not collect

The app does not send your download list, files, clipboard, or identifiers to a VDR server. There is no account. Network traffic goes only to the hosts of the URLs you start. VDR does not upload files and does not use advertising IDs.
